# Cybersecurity-Portfolio
This repository contains documentation of my hands-on security work — including a home lab threat detection project and my experience competing in the National Cyber League CTF.

## Contents

### 🍯 [Honeypot Lab]

Raspberry Pi-based honeypot with a self-hosted Elastic (ELK Stack) SIEM pipeline. Six months of real-world attack data collection, 8 million logs, 14,000 unique IPs, campaign attribution across distributed IPs, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and a formal threat analysis reports.

### 🏆 [NCL Experience]

Reflections on competing in the National Cyber League — preparation methodology, Spring 2026 individual and team results, and NCL Scouting Reports.
